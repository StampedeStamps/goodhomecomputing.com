Who we are

Douglas Boynton founded Good Home Computing in 2001 as a one-man computer repair and maintenance shop. It grew through word of mouth over the years, and some of our original customers still trust us with their computers today.

We take pride in each and every project, because any job worth doing is worth doing well. Our aim is to make computing more accessible and user-friendly for home users, and to stay reliable and affordable for everyone.
